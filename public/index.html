Roster Chorale

Turn any Bach chorale into band parts for the band you actually have: voiced for your roster, in a key that keeps everyone in range, at the grade you pick.

Warming up the arranger…

Step 1 Your roster

One row per student (any column order) or instrument-and-count pairs. Messy names like “Bb Clar”, “Alto Sax 1” or “Tbn” are fine. Nothing is stored on the server.

No roster handy? Download the sample lopsided band.

Step 2 Chorale and grade

The grade sets each instrument's range, which concert keys are allowed, and how much the rhythm is simplified (grade ½–1: eighth notes merged into quarters; grade 2: sixteenths into eighths; grade 3: as written).
